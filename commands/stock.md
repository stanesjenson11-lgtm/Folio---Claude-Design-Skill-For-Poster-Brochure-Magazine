---
description: Find and download on-topic stock photos (free licence) for a subject, with a contact sheet and credits
argument-hint: "<subject, e.g. software studio / turf academy / bakery> [look, e.g. neon, dark, bright yellow]"
---
Use the folio skill to source photos for: $ARGUMENTS

1. List the subject's nouns first (a tech studio → computers, code on screens, phones and apps, robots, servers, people at work with devices; a bakery → bread, ovens, hands with dough…). Imagery comes from the client's world, never generic props.
2. For each noun (plus the wanted look), open the Unsplash search page with WebFetch (`https://unsplash.com/s/photos/<words-with-dashes>`) and ask for free photos only (no Unsplash+), with the direct image URL or the photo id and the photographer's name.
3. Download small previews (`https://images.unsplash.com/photo-<id>?w=400` or `https://unsplash.com/photos/<id>/download?force=true&w=400`), build one contact sheet, and pick with the user's style in mind (hard light for sport styles, neon for pop, white desks for minimal).
4. Download the picks at print size (`w=3200`, or larger for full-bleed covers and cut-outs) into a shared `stock-<subject>/` folder next to the projects, and append one line per photo to `stock-<subject>/CREDITS.txt` (file → photographer).
5. Credit the photographers in the piece; people in stock photos are illustrations, never captioned as the client's team.
