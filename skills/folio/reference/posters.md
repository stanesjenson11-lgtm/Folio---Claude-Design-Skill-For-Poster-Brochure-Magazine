# posters: the design language for church, verse, sports and event posters

Learned from the studio's own body of work: 246 posters (30 Bible-verse posters, 59 portrait event posters, 157 landscape stream thumbnails), studied one by one and rebuilt 1:1 as editable replicas. Read this for every poster or flyer, after `library/TASTE.md` and before `formats.md`. Templates live in the poster kits (`kits/poster-*`, `kit.py list`) once they are installed; this page is the grammar behind them, and until a topic has a kit, folio designs from it directly.

## 1. What a poster has to do
Three reads, in this order, from across a room or a phone feed:
1. **What** — the event or message, the biggest thing on the sheet (cap height ≥ 8% of the height on print, ≥ 12% on a 16:9 thumbnail).
2. **Who** — a face (the speaker, the band, the chief guest, a player) or the symbol of the message (a cross on a hill, hands raised, an open Bible).
3. **When / where / how** — one block: date, time, venue, entry, contact. Never scattered.

Everything else (rails, logos, sponsor, signature, slogans) is small and lives in fixed slots at the edges.

## 2. The layer stack (every poster, back to front)
1. **Ground** — a textured colour: grunge paper, film grain, a soft gradient, kraft, a dark vignette. Never flat.
2. **Big symbol or place** — a huge faint cross, star, arch or sunburst; a venue photo band (the prayer centre dome, the hall); a map; a city skyline. 20–45% contrast.
3. **Atmosphere** — crowd with raised hands, stage lights, light rays, smoke, bokeh, sparks, fireworks; graded to the poster's colour.
4. **Glow** — a soft bloom behind the head and shoulders of the cut-out.
5. **The person** — a cut-out (background removed), 40–60% of the height on portrait, 45–55% of the width on landscape, cropped at the waist or chest, gaze into the sheet. Treatments: full colour · black-and-white on a colour ground · white sticker outline · fade into the ground at the foot.
6. **The headline** — often *behind the head and in front of the ground* (the person cuts the word), or crossing the person's chest; never over the face.
7. **Modules** — date block, venue, speaker credit, cards, ribbons, badges.
8. **Paper devices** — torn-paper strips, masking-tape labels, stamps, stickers, tilted ±3–10°.
9. **Rails and slots** — slogan rail across the top, logos in corners, signature and sponsor at the foot.

## 3. Headline treatments (pick one per poster; vary them across a series)
| # | Treatment | How it is built | Seen on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Script caps + gradient script word** | huge brush-script capitals (Courgette / Satisfy class) with a thin dark outline; a second word in a mint-to-blue gradient script crossing its foot | Thursday Service |
| 2 | **Ultra-condensed tall caps, small word nested** | one word in very tall condensed caps (Six Caps / Antonio / League Gothic), the second word small and bold tucked into its lower counter or under its middle | THURSDAY / SERVICE |
| 3 | **Condensed caps + script overlay** | heavy italic condensed caps (Anton / Bebas / Big Shoulders), a script word in an accent colour written across them | THURSDAY *Service*, SUNDAY *Service* |
| 4 | **Gold 3D serif** | Trajan-class caps (Cinzel) with a metallic gradient fill, a dark extrusion copy behind, a warm glow; two lines centred on a light flare | SERIES NAME / BIBLE STUDY |
| 5 | **Outline repeat stack** | the word repeated 4–8 times in outline or alternating fill, the person cut out between the repeats; the last line solid | SERIES NAME ×6 / BIBLE STUDY |
| 6 | **Ghost word** | the title set enormous at 8–20% opacity behind everything, cut by the person; the readable title smaller in front | session thumbnails |
| 7 | **Script over caps lockup** | a script word ("Have", "Live by") written over spaced or heavy caps ("PURPOSE", "EXAMPLE") | verse posters |
| 8 | **Bilingual lockup** | the Tamil theme word as the hero in a heavy display face (Baloo Thambi / Coiny / Catamaran 900, bevelled or clean) with the English as a spaced-caps gloss, or the reverse | weekly theme-word series, Tamil series titles |
| 9 | **Torn-strip title** | caps stamped on a torn paper strip or a tape label, slightly tilted | speaker credits, CRICKET LEAGUE |
| 10 | **Distressed marker caps** | brush or marker caps with a grunge texture, tilted up to the right, for sport | CRICKET LEAGUE, FOOTBALL MATCH |

Headlines read horizontally (TASTE.md). Vertical text is only for small rails and tabs; tilts are for labels, strips and sport titles, at most 10°.

## 4. Info blocks
- **Date | time split:** a handwritten date ("16.07.2026"), a thin vertical bar, a heavy condensed time ("7PM / ONWARDS").
- **Date badge:** a rounded box with the day huge, month and weekday small, time under it ("OCTOBER / 09 / THURSDAY / 06:30 pm").
- **Stacked date column:** "20 / NOV / 2025 / AT 7PM" down the left edge in heavy caps.
- **Card:** a rounded gradient card (orange → peach) holding a script label ("Worship by") and tracked names.
- **Ribbon list:** numbered rules on alternating ribbons with numbered circles (sport leagues).
- **Pills / chips:** venue, time, entry in rounded pills.
- **Stamp badge:** a round rubber-stamp "FREE ENTRY" or a circle "Entry fee per team ₹600".
- **Venue line:** a pin icon + the venue in caps, the address smaller under it.
- One date format across a series ("Thursday 9 October 2025 · 6:30 pm" or "16.07.2026 | 7PM"), never two on one sheet.

## 5. Series identity (weekly services, session thumbnails)
Fix the rails, vary the body. A series keeps:
- the **top rail** (a three-part slogan in wide white caps, spaced across the top edge);
- the **logo slot** (top right) and partner logos (top row) in the same places;
- the **foot**: signature + role bottom-left ("Secretary, <society>"), the call to action centred ("Come and Be Blessed !"), the sponsor bottom-right ("Dinner sponsored by …");
- the **numbers**: session number and date as a matching pair in two corners (big numeral + script "Session"; numeral + script month);
- a **badge** for live streams ("JOIN US LIVE", a red LIVE tab) in one corner.
The body (headline treatment, colour mood, photo, devices) changes every week, so the feed never repeats itself.

## 6. Genres
### Church service / event (portrait, A4 · A3 · 4:5 · 40 × 60 cm)
Speaker cut-out large on one side or centred; the headline crossing behind the head; "God's Word by / Message by" + name (on tape, a ribbon or a dark bar) near the speaker; date-time block opposite; worship leaders in a card; the foot slots. Big events (anointing day, worship night, convention, anniversary) add: several cut-outs in a group, a partner-logo row, a venue photo band across the foot, a stamp badge, "ALL ARE WELCOME".
### Bible study / sermon thumbnails (landscape 16:9, 1920 × 1080 or 2240 × 1260)
Speaker on the left or right half, eyes about a third down, the title lockup in the other 55%; topic line over a topical collage (places, maps, flags, figures) graded to one mood (sepia-gold, purple, red, teal); session and date in corners; logo in a corner; LIVE badge. Readable at 320 px wide: title cap height ≥ 12% of the height, at most 3 text groups. Keep the bottom-right 15% × 12% clear of key text when the platform overlays a timestamp.
### Bible-verse / theme posters (portrait, social and print)
One full-bleed emotive photo (silhouette at sunrise, hands raised, a cross on a hill, an open Bible, light through fog); the theme lockup in the photo's quiet third (bilingual, script over caps, or the refrain word); the verse itself only when the series quotes it, with the reference; the series tag (the children's ministry name) in one fixed slot. Colour comes from the photo. **Scripture rules in §8.**
### Sports (leagues, matches, tournaments)
Navy or royal blue with grunge (cricket), turf green with lime splashes (football); distressed marker title tilted up; action cut-outs of players; the chief guest portrait in a corner with designation; numbered rules on ribbons; entry-fee stamp; trophy; prizes ribbon; venue · date · time bar; "Register before …" with a phone number. Dense and loud on purpose.
### National days, greetings and fellowships
Republic / Independence Day: white paper, tricolour brush swooshes, a monument illustration over the map, a big numeral with a serif lockup ("77 · The Indian Republic Day"), ceremony time and place, chief guest at the foot. Christmas / New Year: a large script greeting (antlers, stars, fireworks, gold numerals) on a dark ground. Women's or youth fellowship: one colour field, serif italic title, the special speaker under it, date and time in a script line along the foot.

## 7. Type
- **Display:** condensed caps (Anton, Bebas Neue, Oswald, League Gothic, Six Caps, Antonio, Big Shoulders, Saira Extra Condensed), Trajan-class serif (Cinzel), heavy slab/retro (Alfa Slab, Bowlby, Titan One, Bungee), marker (Permanent Marker, Rock Salt).
- **Script accents:** Courgette, Satisfy, Yellowtail, Kaushan Script, Yesteryear, Great Vibes, Allura, Alex Brush, Sacramento, Mrs Saint Delafield, Monsieur La Doulaise.
- **Support:** Montserrat / Poppins / Lexend for venue lines, tracked caps for names, Teko / Barlow Condensed for credits, a hand face (Shadows Into Light, Kalam, Caveat) for the call to action.
- **Tamil:** Baloo Thambi 2 800, Coiny, Catamaran 900 and Anek Tamil 800 for heroes; Hind Madurai, Mukta Malar, Noto Sans Tamil for lines; Arima or Kavivanar for a softer verse. Unicode only (never legacy Bamini).
- One display family + one script + one support face per poster; the script is an accent of 1–3 words, never a paragraph.

## 8. Words
- **Humanize everything folio writes** (`reference/copy.md`): short, concrete, warm; the client's own register and honorifics ("God's Word by", "Come and Be Blessed !", "All are welcome"); no AI tells ("join us for an unforgettable evening of…", triads, "-ing" tails).
- **Facts are sacred:** names, titles (Dr., Rev., Pas., Eva., Bro., Sis.), dates, venues and phone numbers come from the user, spelled exactly as given; check the weekday against the date.
- **Scripture is quoted, never written.** Use the exact text of a named translation (KJV, NKJV, ESV, NIV…; in Tamil the version the church reads, most often the Bible Society of India's Tamil Union Version, பரிசுத்த வேதாகமம், or the Catholic திருவிவிலியம்), with the reference ("John 14:6"). Never paraphrase, shorten inside the quote, merge verses or back-translate between English and Tamil. Given only a reference, fetch the text in the translation the user names (the public-domain KJV when they name none; for Tamil, ask which version) and show it for confirmation before setting it. A theme line that is not a quote ("Have Purpose", "நோக்கம் வேண்டும்") gets no verse reference.
- Tamil and English say the same thing; when only one is supplied, ask before translating, and mark folio's translation for the user to check.

## 9. Making old posters editable
A flat poster (PNG/JPG from Canva or a phone) becomes an editable folio poster with the replica pipeline: `imagery.py fontmatch` (identify each type style) → live HTML text and shapes over the poster → `imagery.py fit --apply` (snap position, size and tracking onto the original) → `imagery.py plate --person --key logo:…` (clean background plate, person cut-out, logo and signature as transparent layers) → `render.py --png-only` → `imagery.py compare` (reference | rebuild | difference). Follow `/folio:replicate`.

## 10. Checks before delivery
- The three reads work at thumbnail size (shrink the PNG to 25%).
- The face is not covered; the headline crosses the person only below the chin or behind the head.
- One info block; one date format; the weekday matches the date.
- Rails, logo, signature and sponsor sit in the series' fixed slots.
- Contrast: every line of text readable on its ground (add a glow, a bar or a gradient behind it rather than shrinking it).
- Canva file ≤ 8 MB and fidelity ≥ 97%; photos and cut-outs are separate layers; words are live.
