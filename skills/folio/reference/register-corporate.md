# register — corporate (annual reports, company profiles, school & NGO reports, prospectuses)

Reader: board members, parents, donors, partners, recruits. They skim, then read two pages closely. Trust is the product: the design must feel deliberate and calm, never salesy, and every number must be right.

**Typical flatplan (16–40 pp):** cover · inside cover/mission · contents · leader's message · about/at a glance · 3–6 themed sections (opener + 1–3 content spreads each) · numbers/financials · people/governance · thanks/partners · back cover with contacts.

**Moves that work (see library):** arc-window or drench cover; quote-portrait message page with the leader's signature; at-a-glance stats spread with 3–5 giant numerals; section openers as full-bleed photo or colour drench with a large section number; mosaic galleries for events; org chart with circular portraits in the motif; data pages on a brand tint with one chart type used consistently; timeline for milestones.

**From the studio's own reports:** section dividers reuse the cover ground so colour marks the chapters; the contents page is a graphic (giant vertical CONTENTS, display numerals) *with page numbers*; numbered figure captions ("fig 3.1 New front elevation") turn snapshots of works into evidence; one grid and one type pair across all sections, never a different template skin per section.

**Tone levers:** one Committed brand colour + tint; generous margins; serif or humanist body for warmth, grotesk for display; photography of real people and places over icons; restrained motion of shapes (one motif).

**Pitfalls:** clip-art icons for values; five different chart styles; walls of mission/vision text set big and centred; stock photos of handshakes; forgetting to credit photos or to mark unaudited numbers; tiny cramped financial tables — give tables air and a proper hierarchy.

**Content integrity is critical here:** names, titles, designations, numbers and dates are checked against the source twice.
