# polish — the last 10% that reads as expensive

Work page by page from the PNGs.
- **Rags.** Ragged-right text should have a gentle, uneven edge without deep holes or a staircase. Fix with `text-wrap: pretty`, small width changes, or a non-breaking space (`&nbsp;`) between short words.
- **Widows and orphans.** No single word on the last line of a paragraph in display or lead text; no single line of a paragraph stranded at the top or bottom of a column. Balance headlines (`text-wrap: balance`) and choose line breaks by sense ("Learning with / Kindness and / Courage").
- **Punctuation.** Real quotes (“ ” ‘ ’), apostrophes (’), en dashes for ranges (2025–26), the client's em or spaced en dashes set consistently (folio's own copy doesn't use dashes to join clauses, see `reference/copy.md`), × for dimensions, ₹ / $ with no space, thin spaces where the house style wants them. Hang opening quote marks in pull quotes.
- **Numbers.** Tabular lining figures in tables and stats; align decimals; same unit style everywhere.
- **Crops.** No cut-off fingertips, heads touching the trim, horizons dead centre, or faces looking off the page. Adjust `--fx/--fy` focal points.
- **Optical alignment.** Big display type needs a small negative left offset (≈ 0.04–0.06em) to look aligned with body text below; round letters and quote marks overshoot.
- **Consistency.** Same caption style, same rule weights (0.25pt hairline, 0.6pt rule), same furniture position, same gap between image and caption on every page.
- **Detail at the edges.** Nothing important within the safe zone; masked shapes and faces away from the spine; bleed on every edge that should bleed.
- **Last look at the flip test** (SKILL.md).
