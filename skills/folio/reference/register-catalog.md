# register — catalog (product & industrial catalogs, quotation/proposal brochures, spec sheets, price lists)

Reader: a buyer or engineer comparing options, often returning to the document. Findability and credibility beat atmosphere, but the best catalogs still feel like a brand (see the marine-airbag reference: every product family gets a photo-band opener, a spec table and a photo strip).

**Typical flatplan:** cover · company introduction · contents with section numbers · per product family: opener (hero photo band + name + one-line promise) → features/spec spread (table, callouts, detail photos) · projects/case studies · certifications/clients · process/how we work · contact/back cover. Quotation brochures add: scope, specification per item, pricing table, terms, validity, signature block.

**Moves that work:** band-stack openers (full-bleed photo top 40–55%, title band, then content); split-panel pages (brand column with vertical title + photo); spec tables with strong header rules and zebra tints in the brand tint; photo strips (4–6 thumbnails on a colour band) for applications; numbered section tabs on the outer edge so the reader can thumb to a family; bilingual headings as a typographic feature (large English, smaller second language) when needed.

**Tone levers:** Committed brand colour as bands and tabs; condensed or wide grotesk display, sturdy sans body; product photography cut out or shot on consistent backgrounds; precise hairline rules.

**Product and data pages** (from the studio's own catalogs and data sheets; see `product-cell-grid`, `spec-sheet-frame`, `layer-section`):
- One opener per product family; sub-families go on band-stack pages. Six full-page openers in 27 pages is too many.
- Every sellable line has a code in one format and position; variants are a hero plus a swatch/thumb row, not repeated cells.
- Price state on every item (price, or "Price on request"), or a separate dated price list so an annual catalog doesn't go stale.
- Specs in 2–3 headed clusters, or 3–4 hero specs pulled out as display numerals (after taste-skill); units stated once per table.
- Technical drawings: keep the client's vectors; balloons keyed to item numbers, dimension letters keyed to table heads; engineering title block (drawing no, revision, date, approval) and the document code in the footer.
- Title colours come from the palette roles, never sampled per photo. Hairline rules, not UI cards and tick icons. Table type 6.5pt minimum.
- A brand mark on the cover and the back cover, always.

**Quotations:** `quote-cover` (client, project, three key numbers, ref, date, validity) → scope → specs → `estimate-sheet` (qty/unit/rate lines, ₹ with Indian grouping, GST, total in words, bank details, signature) → terms with tax and validity first. Facts (counts of projects, years, staff, web address) match the client's FACTS.md across every piece.

**Pitfalls:** tables with every border drawn; inconsistent units (mm vs cm); different photo backgrounds per product; prices without validity dates or GST notes; spec numbers invented or rounded without permission.
