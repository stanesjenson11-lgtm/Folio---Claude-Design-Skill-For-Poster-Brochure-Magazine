Mango Inc. engineers web and mobile applications, custom software, cloud infrastructure and AI automation, and owns every project from the first sketch through deployment and support.

A landing page goes live in one to two weeks, a full website with a content system in three to five, and a mobile app or software platform in eight to fourteen. Every timeline is agreed before work begins.

Basic websites start at $2,500, and every website comes with one month of support after launch, while real visitors arrive and the first changes come in.

Websites, stores, apps and AI, from one studio in Springfield.

Every website is fast, secure and responsive: an editable content system, clean metadata, accessibility and Core Web Vitals tuning come as standard, on Astro, Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS or WordPress.

Online stores add secure checkout, product catalogues, order and inventory management and an admin dashboard, with three months of support after launch.
