Work That Ships On Time

Written by the Mango Inc. studio

A landing page goes live in one to two weeks, a full website with a content system in three to five weeks, and a mobile app or software platform in eight to fourteen. Basic websites start at $2,500, and every other project is quoted on its scope.

The timeline is agreed before work begins. Then one team plans, designs, builds and deploys the project, and stays on for support after launch: one month for every website, three months for every online store, and a bug-fix window for every app.

Every website is fast, secure and responsive, with an editable content system, clean metadata, accessibility and Core Web Vitals tuning. Online stores add secure checkout, product catalogues, order and inventory management and an admin dashboard. Apps reach the App Store and Play Store with push notifications, REST API integration and UI and UX design included.

Clients range from e-commerce and retail to healthcare, education, real estate, travel, finance and logistics.

Ten Projects Delivered Across the US

Mango Inc. has delivered ten client projects across the US, in e-commerce and retail, healthcare and wellness, corporate and business, and education and e-learning.

Cloud, APIs and AI Behind Every Launch

Cloud infrastructure runs on AWS, Azure or Google Cloud, with Docker, Kubernetes and automated delivery pipelines, so every release ships the same careful way and a rollback is always one step away.

API and systems integration connects new software to the tools a business already uses, from payment gateways to inventory and accounts.

AI agents answer calls and messages around the clock and hand over to people when it matters, built as production systems rather than demos.

Connecting your systems to your customers

Online stores connect to payment gateways, inventory and accounts, so an order placed at midnight is invoiced, packed and tracked by morning without anyone retyping it.

Mobile apps share the same cloud and APIs as the website, so customers see the same account, orders and notifications wherever they sign in, on Android, iOS or the web.
