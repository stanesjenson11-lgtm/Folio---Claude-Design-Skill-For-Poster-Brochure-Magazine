# Taste profile

What this user has loved, liked and rejected, newest first. Folio reads it at every intake and before every variant set, and rewrites it after every verdict (`reference/taste.md`). Defaults below win over the library's general rules; the log is the evidence.

## Defaults (derived from the log)
- **Posters:** build in the `arc-stage` direction. Four variants means four variations *inside* that family (different heading font, header position, colours, texture, info block), not four unrelated styles.
- **Headlines:** horizontal on posters, books and brochures. Magazines may also set BOLD words vertically, reading top to bottom (90° clockwise), never upside-down or bottom-to-top (preflight `rotated-heading`; mark the page or `<html>` with `data-allow-vertical`).
- **Photo and author frames:** plain rectangles, portrait or square. No wave, blob, circle or other curved photo masks unless the user asks or a reference being replicated uses them. Not every image soft-edged: mix sketches with plain rectangles.
- **People pages:** every named person gets a portrait frame; when no photo is supplied, a placeholder with the name, ready for the photo in Canva: circles in magazines, plain rectangles in books and posters.
- **Curves** belong in the ground and the small furniture: concentric arcs or rings, soft blobs behind a photo, rounded info cards and pills.
- **Fonts:** aesthetic faces only on posters; on brochures for headers, subheaders, cover, back cover and contents (body may be Times New Roman). Roster in `typography.md`.
- **Poster grounds:** layered, never flat: glow blooms, the family texture, fine dots, low-opacity paper grain.
- **Colour:** the user picks the theme colour; keep palettes aesthetic, and not pastel by default: vivid contrast pairings (teal + orange, ink + saffron, black + electric) are welcome; no raw yellow, green or purple.
- **Magazines: two loved lanes, offer both.** `sport-layers` **Ivory**: exactly the studio magazine v1 (espresso cover with a cut-out person, ivory reading pages with a faint shaded photo ground, champagne gutter boxes, sage bands, Big Shoulders words, pencil sketches across the fold): "PERFECT", keep it exactly. `sport-layers` **Night**: mostly dark grounds with light and vivid accents, strong contrast, BOLD words horizontally and vertically: the user wants more of this. Classic luxury and indie minimalism are book styles.
- **Style option "Old-money classic book"** (`old-money-book`): the studio booklet look (Ivory, Espresso, Champagne & Sage, sharp serif, sage heads, pencil sketches). Loved for books and normal brochures.
- **Premium copy from thin input:** write confident, specific, benefit-led lines (on-time delivery, the finish, past clients, the industries served), humanized; never filler and never invented numbers.
- **Tech pieces show the real logos** of the technologies and platforms the client uses (`imagery.py logos`), in one ink colour, on services and stack pages.
- **Every spread connects across the fold** in magazines: a note box, band, photo or cut-out that runs from the left page into the right. Pages are layered (paper grain, background words, tinted panels), never plain white, and as dense as the reference.
- **No empty areas:** a colour band or panel holding one line gets supporting content (facts, a short process, logos, a sketch) or is made smaller.
- **Imagery comes from the client's world:** a tech company gets computers, its websites and apps on devices, AI, robots, code and circuits; never generic lifestyle props (coffee cups, notebooks) as the main images.
- **Two-page colour areas meet with a flowing edge** (a wave), not a straight dividing line (`flow-divide`).
- **Back pages are designed:** a closing composition (image or layers, the motif, a sign-off) around the contacts, never a plain list on a flat ground.
- **Style catalogue (kits):** most clients bring content and a theme colour, not references. Offer the kits in `kits/INDEX.md` (`/folio:styles`, or the named style commands), start one with `kit.py new`, fill it with premium copy and on-topic images. Every signed-off replica becomes a kit (`kit.py save`).
- **Reference replicas:** when the user gives reference pages and asks for 1:1, build every page and spread the reference shows (not a selection), and match the page proportions, grid, colour blocking, type weight and scale and devices exactly; swap in the client's content and on-topic images. Compare side by side (reference crop | ours) before delivering.
- **No decorative rules:** no hairlines under list items or headings, above text, or as spacers over blank space (preflight `rule-heavy`). Separate with space, type size and colour fields.
- **Brochure images:** small coloured-pencil sketches (`imagery.py sketch`), frameless with soft hand-finished edges, placed next to the text they belong to. Posters keep plain rectangular photos.
- **Colour count:** 3–4 visible colours, never 2 (preflight `palette-thin`): paper, ink/field, accent, support.
- **Lists become spreads:** a list of offerings with no images is a two-page spread with a sketch beside each group, not one stacked page.
- **Magazines:** sports-magazine layering (`sport-layers`): the cover stacks the context background, a cut-out person, a hero object, a motion streak, a spark and a newsprint overlay; images layered in depth (not a collage grid); continuity through note boxes that stretch from the left page to the right (`gutter-box`, loved) and elements across the gutter.
- **Avoid by default:** liquid wave bands, pencil water-line textures, rotated headlines, decorative rules.
