# Civic Arc

**Essence:** Institutional warmth: one saturated blue (or the client's colour) as giant arcs over architectural photography, light-to-heavy grotesk titles, lots of white.

**Colour strategy:** Committed — brand colour on ~40% of cover/openers, tint panels inside, white text pages. Re-colour with the client's brand; keep the *amount* and *placement* of colour, not the hue.

**Type:** Display: a grotesk with a real Light and Bold (Hanken Grotesk, Red Hat Display, Schibsted Grotesk); body: a calm serif or humanist sans (Source Serif 4, Red Hat Text). Titles mix Light year + Bold word. Verify against `reference/typography.md`; client brand fonts override.

**Motif:** Circle/arc — cover crop, portrait masks, stat circles, back cover window.

**Photography:** Natural colour, architecture and people outdoors, sky visible; slightly cool grade.

**Pattern set:** arc-window, quote-portrait, stat-spread, mosaic, numbered-index, circle-portraits, back-cover

**Fits:** Schools, colleges, NGOs, hospitals, foundations, welfare societies.

**Watch out:** the direction is a lane, not a template — change proportions, crops and the motif's details for each client so two books in this lane don't look the same.
