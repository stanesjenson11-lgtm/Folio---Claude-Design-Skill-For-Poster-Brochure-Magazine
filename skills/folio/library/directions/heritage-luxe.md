# Heritage Luxe

**Essence:** Classic luxury editorial. Stark contrast, generous margins, one expensive colour, photography framed like a portrait sitting. Confident and quiet at the same time.

**Palettes:** `p30` Black, Crimson & Ivory · `p31` Deep Emerald & Champagne · `p32` Burgundy & Soft Charcoal (`data/palettes.csv`). Ivory, never pure white.

**Type:** A sharp editorial serif for mastheads, headers and numerals (Aesthet Nova → stand-in Bodoni Moda; The Seasons → Gloock), very large on covers and openers, tight tracking; small caps or widely spaced caps for labels (Wayfinder CF → Familjen Grotesk). Body in Times New Roman at 9.5–10pt, justified with hyphenation, or ragged for short texts.

**Motif:** a masthead lockup repeated small on every page and big italic serif pull lines. No decorative rules (user taste): separation comes from space, type scale and one colour field per spread.

**Photography:** black-and-white or rich colour portraits, full-bleed openers, one image per spread; rectangular frames.

**Pattern set:** full-bleed-opener, quote-portrait, numbered-index, two-tone-headline, back-cover

**Fits:** fashion, jewellery, hotels, weddings, galas, culture, premium real estate, anniversary books.

**Watch out:** luxury is restraint: one accent colour, no gradients, no shadows, no more than two faces.
