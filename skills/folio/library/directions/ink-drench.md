# Ink Drench

**Essence:** Confident finance: covers and openers drenched in deep ultramarine, one mint/fresh accent reserved for numbers, ghost words for scale, human photography inside.

**Colour strategy:** Drenched covers + Committed interiors (brand column on every spread) + one accent only on numerals. Re-colour with the client's brand; keep the *amount* and *placement* of colour, not the hue.

**Type:** Display: a modern grotesk in caps with light/bold pairs (Schibsted Grotesk, Onest); body: Source Serif 4 or Figtree. Numbers large in the accent. Verify against `reference/typography.md`; client brand fonts override.

**Motif:** Ghost type + brand column — faint giant words, a brand panel on one side of each spread.

**Photography:** Warm documentary portraits (families, customers, staff) to counter the cool colour.

**Pattern set:** drench-cover, quote-portrait, ghost-type, stat-spread, data-tint, map-reach, numbered-sequence, circle-portraits

**Fits:** Insurance, banking, investment, fintech, co-operative societies.

**Watch out:** the direction is a lane, not a template — change proportions, crops and the motif's details for each client so two books in this lane don't look the same.
