# Pop Stripe

**Essence:** Bright landscape pages cut into vertical stripes (white, light grey, saffron), with photos in circles and rounded squares that overlap the stripe edges and cast a soft mirror reflection. Friendly, glossy, pop.

**Page:** 16:9 landscape. Stripes run the full height: a narrow white strip, a wide grey panel for text, a saffron block for imagery. Logo mark in a corner; three dots as a pager.

**Colour strategy:** saffron `#FFC20E`, light grey `#E4E5E9`, white, ink `#1D1D1F`, with an orange-to-saffron gradient only on the small stacked cards.

**Type:** eyebrow in a small sans ("About …"), a two-line serif head (Aesthet Nova → Bodoni Moda or The Seasons → Gloock), spaced bold caps label, grey body.

**Motif:** pairs of small gradient rounded cards, one tall and one short, overlapping a stripe edge; icon bullets in saffron discs with a small offset dot.

**Photography:** objects on saffron, circle and rounded-square crops, a faded mirror reflection under the photo (`imagery.py reflect`).

**Pattern set:** split-panel, circle-portraits, back-cover

**Watch out:** reflections and gradients stay on imagery and the small cards, never on text; body text sits on grey or white.
