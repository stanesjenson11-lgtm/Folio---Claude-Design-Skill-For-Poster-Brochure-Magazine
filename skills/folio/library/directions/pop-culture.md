# Pop Culture

**Essence:** Loud, nostalgic, stylised. Slightly desaturated retro colour, huge type that collides with photos, stickers and tags, energy from contrast rather than from clutter.

**Palettes:** `p33` Mustard Yellow & Vintage Black · `p34` Faded Terracotta & Teal · `p35` Muted Lavender & Acid Lime.

**Type:** Big display faces (Hagrid → Chonburi; Fromage → Young Serif; Wanchy/Shameless → Shrikhand) overlapping photos; hand-lettered tags (Sedgwick Ave); labels in a characterful sans (Wayfinder CF → Familjen Grotesk). Body in Times New Roman or Arial, short columns.

**Motif:** sticker badges, tape strips, halftone dots and paper grain (`imagery.py motif --type dots`, `texture --kind grain`), one rotated sticker per spread (never a rotated headline).

**Photography:** high-contrast colour or duotone in the palette, cut-outs allowed, rectangular frames with hard crops.

**Pattern set:** cutout-masthead, full-bleed-opener, two-tone-headline, numbered-sequence, mosaic

**Fits:** music, festivals, youth programmes, streetwear, galleries, sports culture, launches.

**Watch out:** retro colour is desaturated, not neon; keep body text calm so the loud parts stay loud.
