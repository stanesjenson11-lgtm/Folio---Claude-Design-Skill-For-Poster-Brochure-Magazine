# Night Court

**Essence:** Sports magazine at night: black and near-black pages alternating with white pages, amber accent on the key word, side-lit athletes and cut-outs, pill tags.

**Colour strategy:** Committed dark: ~50% of pages near-black; accent amber only on key words and small marks. Re-colour with the client's brand; keep the *amount* and *placement* of colour, not the hue.

**Type:** Display: condensed bold caps, occasionally italic (Barlow Condensed 800, Saira Extra Condensed); body: Barlow or Commissioner. Two-tone headlines everywhere. Verify against `reference/typography.md`; client brand fonts override.

**Motif:** Plus-sign mark + rounded photo frames used consistently (the book's own motif).

**Photography:** Dramatic low-key lighting, dark backgrounds, sweat and texture; cut-outs over type.

**Pattern set:** cutout-masthead, full-bleed-opener, two-tone-headline, numbered-index, quote-portrait, mosaic

**Fits:** Sports clubs, academies, turf/sports infrastructure, events, fitness brands.

**Watch out:** black + one hot accent is a place generated design lands by default (direction.md calibration): earn it with the client's brand and real night photography, and keep the accent-word headline to openers. The direction is a lane, not a template — change proportions, crops and the motif's details for each client so two books in this lane don't look the same.
