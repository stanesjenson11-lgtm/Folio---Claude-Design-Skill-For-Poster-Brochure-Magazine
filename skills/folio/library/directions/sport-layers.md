# Sport Layers

**Essence:** Sports-magazine energy for any subject: dark grounds, loud contrast, a person bursting off the cover, images stacked in depth, BOLD words set horizontally and vertically, big numbers as proof, and notes that travel across the spread.

**Two lanes (offer both):**
- **Ivory** (user-loved, "PERFECT", keep exactly): palette `p42` Ivory, Espresso, Champagne & Sage. Espresso cover and openers with a faint duotone photo ground and grain, ivory reading pages with a faint shaded photo ground shared across each spread, champagne gutter boxes, sage bands with big ivory numerals, Big Shoulders words in espresso, coloured-pencil sketches across the fold. Reference build: the studio magazine.
- **Night** (more contrast, the user wants more of this): mostly dark grounds (near-black, deep navy or charcoal) with light and vivid accents: one electric accent for numbers and streaks, one warm light for type, one support colour for gutter boxes and fields.

**Type (both lanes):** condensed heavy display from the aesthetic roster (Big Shoulders Display, Sofia Sans Extra Condensed, League Gothic) at huge sizes, horizontal and vertical (reading top to bottom, `data-allow-vertical`), outline words behind the figure; labels in Wayfinder CF → Familjen Grotesk; body in Times New Roman.

**Cover stack (Night lane; the Ivory lane uses ground, masthead, colour field, photo, person, cover lines) (back to front, `cutout-layers`):** 1 context background from the client's world (a court for sport; a server room, code wall or city lights for tech), graded into the dark ground; 2 masthead; 3 cut-out person; 4 hero object (a ball; for tech a laptop, phone, robot or chip); 5 motion streak that follows the object (air flow, light trail); 6 spark or flare; 7 newsprint or halftone overlay at low opacity; 8 cover lines and big numerals.

**Motif:** layers, notes across the gutter (`gutter-box`), colour areas that meet across a spread with a flowing edge (`flow-divide`), big numerals.

**Photography:** on-topic only (tech → devices, screens, code, robots, circuits); one cut-out person per piece; rectangles elsewhere.

**Pattern set:** cutout-layers, gutter-box, flow-divide, stat-spread, vertical-title, back-cover

**Fits:** tech studios, sports clubs, events, youth brands, launches.

**Watch out:** no rules, no drop shadows, never upside-down type; keep one calmer reading page per spread (still dark).
