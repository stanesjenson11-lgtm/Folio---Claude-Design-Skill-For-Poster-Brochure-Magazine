# Split Pop

**Essence:** Landscape portfolio pages built from hard colour splits: a narrow side rail, a big field in the second colour, white reading panels, objects-on-colour photos and device mockups. Loud, clean, commercial.

**Page:** 16:9 landscape (e.g. 320 × 180 mm). Side rail ≈ 17.5% of the width in white, orange or teal, with the page number (bold) top-left, a year line mid-left, a two-bar mark at the foot and one thin vertical line at ≈ 13% of the width. Some pages add a right strip split into two colours.

**Colour strategy:** teal `#12B5B0` + orange `#FF7A0A` + white + ink `#222`, as big flat fields; photos graded into the orange (duotone ink → orange → peach) so objects sit on one colour.

**Type:** heavy geometric caps for the page title with a colour disc behind the first letter (`letter-disc-title`); spaced bold caps for labels ("PROJECT 01"); a light sans body (Arial on request, else Times New Roman).

**Motif:** hatched shapes (circle, rounded square, corner bracket, half-disc: `imagery.py hatch`), solid dots, striped-triangle and chevron bullet marks.

**Photography:** objects on a single colour, circle crops for heroes, tight grids of 2–6 with small gaps, phone / tablet / desktop mockups showing the client's own screens (`device-mockup`).

**Pattern set:** letter-disc-title, device-mockup, split-panel, mosaic, back-cover

**Watch out:** one title per page; the thin line after each title in the source is a decorative rule, so leave it out (user rule).
