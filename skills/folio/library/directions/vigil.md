# Vigil

**Essence:** Faith and welfare work at dusk: deep navy, plum or near-black grounds, one warm light (amber or gold), backlit photography and the organisation's mark used large. Ceremonial on openers, orderly inside.

**Colour strategy:** Drenched for cover, dividers and back cover (a flat ground, or the client's own established gradient, reused so colour marks the chapters); Restrained white interiors with the accent only on numerals, rules and pull quotes. Re-colour with the client's brand; keep the *amount* and *placement* of colour, not the hue.

**Type:** Display: widely tracked serif caps for names and titles (Marcellus, Cinzel; check the reflex list in typography.md) paired with one heavy condensed face for numerals and section words; body: a calm humanist sans or serif. Tamil through `bilingual-lockup`. Two families, not six.

**Motif:** The organisation's own mark (emblem, halo, cross) used large, recoloured or ghosted at 5–10%; a fine texture on dark grounds (contour lines, grain) at very low contrast.

**Photography:** Backlit and dusk light; the community's real people and buildings; documentary photos graded to one warm tone (`imagery.py grade`) so phone shots sit together.

**Pattern set:** drench-cover (also as section dividers), vertical-title, numbered-index, quote-portrait, mosaic, speaker-poster, series-rail, numeral-window, back-cover

**Fits:** Churches, ministries, welfare societies, trusts, anniversary books, faith events.

**Watch out:** the lane invites template decoration (filigree, florals, doves, praying hands, glow text). Use the organisation's own mark and real photographs instead. Centred symmetry belongs on openers; interiors use the grid.
