# Indie Quiet

**Essence:** Slow, intellectual, calm. Muted organic tones, very wide margins, small type, big air, photographs of objects and places in soft daylight.

**Palettes:** `p27` Warm Oatmeal & Espresso · `p28` Olive Green & Soft Linen · `p29` Slate Blue & Chalk.

**Type:** A soft serif for titles (The Seasons → Gloock; Fromage → Young Serif) set surprisingly small for a title, sentence case; labels in a characterful sans in small caps (Wayfinder CF → Familjen Grotesk); body in Times New Roman 9–9.5pt with generous leading. Numbers as text, not graphics.

**Motif:** nothing decorative: the grid, the margins and generous space. No hairlines (user taste). Page numbers and running heads set small and far from the text block.

**Photography:** natural light, muted colour, lots of negative space inside the frame; rectangular frames, often small on a large page.

**Pattern set:** full-bleed-opener (sparingly), quote-portrait, mosaic (loose, with air), numbered-index, back-cover

**Fits:** architecture, interiors, travel, slow living, cafés, ceramics, boutique hotels, design studios.

**Watch out:** minimal is not empty: every page still has one decisive element (a photo, a quote, a title) and its foot is a decision.
