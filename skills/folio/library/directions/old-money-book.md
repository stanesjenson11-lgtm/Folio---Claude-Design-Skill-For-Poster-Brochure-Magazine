# Old-Money Classic Book

**Essence:** A quiet, expensive company book. Ivory paper, espresso type and fields, champagne seals and boxes, sage colour areas; a sharp editorial serif; coloured-pencil sketches beside the text; note boxes that travel across the fold.

**Colour strategy:** palette `p42` Ivory, Espresso, Champagne & Sage, all four visible. Sage carries large type only; champagne carries small text.

**Type:** Aesthet Nova → Bodoni Moda for heads, numerals and the masthead (italic for openers); Wayfinder CF → Familjen Grotesk spaced caps for labels; Times New Roman body.

**Motif:** coloured-pencil sketches (`sketch-spot`), `gutter-box`, colour heads and bands that meet across a spread with a flowing edge (`flow-divide`).

**Photography:** sketches from on-topic photos, mixed with plain rectangles; rectangular portraits for people (placeholders when no photo).

**Pattern set:** sketch-spot, gutter-box, flow-divide, stat-spread, numbered-index, back-cover

**Fits:** studio and company profiles, anniversary books, heritage businesses, consultancies, hotels, jewellers.

**Watch out:** for magazines offer `sport-layers` (Ivory or Night) instead; no hairline rules; the back page needs a composition, not a contact list; people pages need portrait frames or placeholders.
