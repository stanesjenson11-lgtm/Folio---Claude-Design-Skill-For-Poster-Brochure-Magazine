# Flow

**Essence:** No boxes. Colour moves in liquid bands and organic blobs, photos sit in wave, blob or circle frames, and hand-drawn pencil water lines add texture. One shape family per piece, drawn from the brand palette.

**Colour strategy:** Committed or Drenched grounds with 2–3 band/blob colours from the palette; text colours checked against the band they sit on (keep text off wave crests: set `--level` low enough).

**Type:** One expressive display face per piece (see `typography.md` → Poster pairs) + a calm body; Tamil through `bilingual-lockup`.

**Motif:** `imagery.py motif --type liquid | blob | pencil`, or `arcs` + a circle window. Photo frames from `imagery.py mask`. Rounded pills and cards (`data-allow-round`) for small info.

**Photography:** real people and places in plain rectangular frames (user taste, 2026-09-29); liquid bands and pencil lines were rejected as poster defaults, so use them only on request.

**Pattern set:** speaker-poster, bilingual-lockup, numeral-window, quote-portrait, back-cover

**Fits:** Events, churches, youth programmes, wellness, hospitality, travel, anything the user wants to feel soft and moving rather than architectural.

**Watch out:** Flow turns into clip-art when shapes are random: every band or blob sits behind or under something on purpose, colours come from the palette, and the grid still aligns the text. The full style card lands in `reference/styles.md`.
