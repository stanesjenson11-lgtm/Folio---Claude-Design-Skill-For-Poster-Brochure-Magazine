# Harbor Band

**Essence:** Industrial catalog as a system: every product family opens with a full-bleed photo band and the same title position, spec tables and photo strips, optional bilingual headings.

**Colour strategy:** Committed — ocean/brand blue bands, white content zones, blue tint table stripes. Re-colour with the client's brand; keep the *amount* and *placement* of colour, not the hue.

**Type:** Display: wide or regular grotesk in caps (Archivo 800, Archivo Expanded, Rethink Sans); body: sturdy sans (Archivo, Public Sans). Second language smaller and lighter. Verify against `reference/typography.md`; client brand fonts override.

**Motif:** Horizontal band — photo bands, title bands, bottom photo strips, section tabs.

**Photography:** Product-in-use photography at scale (ships, sites, machinery), consistent daylight; product close-ups on neutral ground.

**Pattern set:** band-stack, split-panel, photo-strip, mosaic, data-tint, numbered-index, back-cover

**Fits:** Manufacturers, industrial suppliers, pipe fittings, construction products, quotation brochures.

**Watch out:** the direction is a lane, not a template — change proportions, crops and the motif's details for each client so two books in this lane don't look the same.
