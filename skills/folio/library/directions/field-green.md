# Field Green

**Essence:** Friendly services brochure: a fresh single colour as offset blocks behind photos, rounded-free rectangles, two-tone headlines, abundant photography.

**Colour strategy:** Committed — lime/brand panels on ~30% of surfaces, white elsewhere. Re-colour with the client's brand; keep the *amount* and *placement* of colour, not the hue.

**Type:** Display: a characterful grotesk (Bricolage Grotesque, Familjen Grotesk) bold caps; body: Figtree or Karla. Verify against `reference/typography.md`; client brand fonts override.

**Motif:** Offset block — colour rectangle shifted behind every photo.

**Photography:** Bright natural daylight, hands and produce close up, people working.

**Pattern set:** offset-block, split-panel, two-tone-headline, full-bleed-opener, mosaic, back-cover

**Fits:** Agriculture, food, wellness, education services, local businesses.

**Watch out:** the direction is a lane, not a template — change proportions, crops and the motif's details for each client so two books in this lane don't look the same.
