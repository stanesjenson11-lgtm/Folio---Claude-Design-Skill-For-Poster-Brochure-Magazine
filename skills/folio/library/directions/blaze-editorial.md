# Blaze Editorial

**Essence:** Fitness editorial: blaze orange + black + warm light grey paper, ultra-heavy wide headlines that overlap photos, stacked outline type, generous body columns.

**Colour strategy:** Committed accent — orange on headline words and one drenched cover; pages mostly light grey with black type. Re-colour with the client's brand; keep the *amount* and *placement* of colour, not the hue.

**Type:** Display: ultra-heavy wide grotesk (Unbounded 800, Dela Gothic One, Archivo Black); body: Hanken Grotesk or Karla. Mixed-weight headlines ('WAKE UP. WORK OUT. / REPEAT'). Verify against `reference/typography.md`; client brand fonts override.

**Motif:** Overlap — headlines overlapping photo edges; circular inset photos.

**Photography:** Bright studio and gym photography, cut-outs, food flat-lays for nutrition pages.

**Pattern set:** cutout-masthead, two-tone-headline, full-bleed-opener, numbered-sequence, ghost-type, quote-portrait

**Fits:** Gyms, fitness coaches, sports nutrition, lifestyle brands.

**Watch out:** the direction is a lane, not a template — change proportions, crops and the motif's details for each client so two books in this lane don't look the same.
