# Signal Yellow

**Essence:** Tabloid energy: deep navy/black with acid yellow blocks, vertical labels, arrow bullets, dense grids of action photos.

**Colour strategy:** Full palette of two: dark base + acid accent on large blocks (not just text). Re-colour with the client's brand; keep the *amount* and *placement* of colour, not the hue.

**Type:** Display: squared or techno grotesk bold caps (Chakra Petch, Saira, Rubik 800); body: Rubik or Public Sans. Verify against `reference/typography.md`; client brand fonts override.

**Motif:** Yellow slab + vertical label — blocks that frame photos and hold spine titles.

**Photography:** Action freeze-frames, stadium light, high saturation.

**Pattern set:** cutout-masthead, vertical-title, mosaic, split-panel, numbered-index

**Fits:** Sports media, youth events, gyms, e-sports, promotions.

**Watch out:** the direction is a lane, not a template — change proportions, crops and the motif's details for each client so two books in this lane don't look the same.
