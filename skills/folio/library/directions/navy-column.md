# Navy Column

**Essence:** Architectural corporate: deep navy slabs and columns, very heavy condensed caps, black-and-white or cool photos of buildings and interiors, vertical spine titles.

**Colour strategy:** Committed — navy slabs and full navy pages alternate with white; almost no second colour. Re-colour with the client's brand; keep the *amount* and *placement* of colour, not the hue.

**Type:** Display: heavy condensed caps (Big Shoulders Display, Barlow Condensed 800, League Gothic); body: a neutral sans (Barlow, Public Sans). Section titles often vertical. Verify against `reference/typography.md`; client brand fonts override.

**Motif:** Vertical slab/column — cover slabs, spine titles, navy side columns, tab markers.

**Photography:** Desaturated or B&W architecture, looking up; interiors; people in context, never posed handshakes.

**Pattern set:** slab-cover, vertical-title, split-panel, numbered-index, data-tint, map-reach, quote-portrait

**Fits:** Consultancies, construction, real estate, finance, B2B services.

**Watch out:** the direction is a lane, not a template — change proportions, crops and the motif's details for each client so two books in this lane don't look the same.
