# Web references: Arc family (2026-09)

Study material only. These notes describe grammar and never the artwork itself. All images are Behance previews, 1000 px or less.

## 2026-09-ceia-crossing-foot.jpg
- Source: https://www.behance.net/gallery/242423153/Social-Media-Church-069-Way-House-Church. Designer: Oséias Gondim.
- Shared: a dark ground, a central speaker, a huge headline over the torso, and a tracked date/time line centred below.
- Moves:
  - Put an accent hairline rectangle behind the portrait, larger than it and offset up.
  - Hang a small light kicker ("DOMINGO DE") off the headline's first cap.
  - End the info with a short accent rule.
- Avoid: the wide grotesk headline (it loses the condensed height) and the bokeh ground.

## 2026-09-plenitude-numeral-badge.jpg
- Source: https://www.behance.net/gallery/248450339/Projet-de-concert-gospel. Designer: Franck Remy Ona.
- Shared: a condensed headline crossing the photo's foot, a tracked sub-line and a stacked date numeral.
- Moves:
  - Greyscale photo on a coloured ground.
  - Track the sub-line to exactly the headline's width.
  - Flank the headline with equal-height badges (venue left, "24 / MAI / 2026" right).
- Avoid: the light-streak ground, the multicolour price pills and the outline type.

## 2026-09-night-of-worship-mono.jpg
- Source: https://www.behance.net/gallery/249399033/Night-of-Worship-Poster-and-Social-Media-Campaign. Designer: Angeo Basson.
- Shared: a single-hue ground, a condensed headline overlapping the photo and a centred info block.
- Moves:
  - Grade the photo into the ground hue.
  - Put paper grain over everything, type included.
  - Set the connector word between hairlines ("— OF —").
  - Use the accent only for the name and CTA.
- Avoid: the left-aligned stack and the crowd photo at the foot.

## 2026-09-domingo-de-gloria.jpg
- Source: https://www.behance.net/gallery/252093727/SOCIAL-MEDIA-IGLESIA-FLYERS-CRISTIANOS-2. Designer: Daniel Hernandez.
- Shared: a massive two-line condensed headline crossing the lower photo, and a centred tracked address.
- Moves:
  - Tint the headline where it overlaps the photo so the photo shows faintly through; letters stay solid over the ground.
  - Set date and time as a symmetric left/right pair on one baseline.
- Avoid: the headline bleeding off the edge and the tiny second cutout figure.

## 2026-09-radial-burst-matchday.jpg
- Source: https://www.behance.net/gallery/252023433/World-Cup-2026. Designer: Matheus Correa (sports).
- Shared: a centred symmetric composition, a single-hue ground and a radial line texture.
- Moves:
  - Centre the radial texture on the head, not the page.
  - Draw the texture in low-opacity accent over the ground hue.
  - Put grain on the ground.
- Avoid: grunge brush, the script tag, the cutout and the headline hidden behind the head.

## 2026-09-welcoming-service-grain.jpg
- Source: https://www.behance.net/gallery/256011967/COLLEGE-CHURCH-DESIGN-. Designer: Kingsley Mtunduwatha.
- Shared: kicker + red condensed "WELCOMING SERVICE" behind the speaker, with the credit bottom-left.
- Moves:
  - Fill the second word with crisp mechanical hatching.
  - Stack the date and time left and right of the headline.
  - Keep the speaker in colour and the rest in greyscale.
- Avoid: the torn-paper edge. Hatching must never look pencil-drawn.

## Moves to add to arc-stage
- Centre the rings on the speaker's head, in low-opacity accent. *(radial-burst)*
- Headline translucent only where it crosses the photo and solid over the ground. Legibility first. *(domingo-de-gloria)*
- Duotone the rectangular portrait into the ground hue; the accent is kept for the headline, card and CTA. *(night-of-worship, plenitude)*
- Accent hairline rectangle, larger than the photo, offset up behind it. It stays rectangular. *(ceia)*
- Sub-line tracked to the headline's width, flanked by hairlines: "— S E R V I C E —". *(plenitude, night-of-worship)*
- Paper grain at about 5% over ground, photo and type. *(night-of-worship, welcoming)*

## Variant ideas
1. **Arc Keyline**: photo inside an offset accent keyline rectangle, with the rings reduced to two or three arcs. The headline crosses both and the corner card stays.
2. **Arc Lockup**: no corner card. The headline is flanked by two equal-height rounded badges (venue left, date numeral right) forming one bar across the photo foot. The credit moves bottom-centre.
3. **Arc Monotone**: ground, photo and rings in one hue with grain, and a paper-white headline. The accent appears only in the card and CTA; the rings are near tone-on-tone.
